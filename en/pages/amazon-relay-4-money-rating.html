8. How the money works

What it is

Money in Relay is simpler than with brokers. No rate confirmation in your inbox, no invoice to raise, no waiting thirty to forty-five days and no factoring taking its percentage. The other side of that coin: the price is not negotiable, deadhead is never paid, and accessorials like detention and truck-ordered-not-used are opaque and have to be claimed through cases.

What the rate is made of

ComponentHow it is setWhat it means for you
Base rateAmazon sets it by lane, demand and urgency. It moves hour to hour, and Hot Loads carry a premium.The same pair of facilities can pay differently in the morning and in the evening. Do not memorise a lane price, read the board.
Fuel surchargeIncluded in the all-in figure under Relay's own program policies. Not shown separately, not negotiable.When comparing with a broker, remember their fuel is often quoted separately while Relay's is already inside.
TollsIncluded in the all-in figure.On Northeast lanes that is a real chunk of the rate. Keep a transponder in the truck; you do not collect receipts.
DeadheadNever paid.The main hidden cost. Price the whole loop, not the loaded leg.
Block payA block is paid in full for the time, even if Amazon did not use every hour.An underused block is profit, not loss: the driver rests and the money is the same.
NegotiationNone, with three exceptions: Post A Truck where you set the minimum, auctions where you name a price, and Relay Assistant offering a counter-offer on urgent loads to high-grade carriers.Do not call support asking for more money; that mechanism does not exist.

When the money lands

  • No fees. Not for registration, not for booking, not for the payout.
  • Factoring is optional. A weekly payment from Amazon is more reliable than most brokers. Paying a factor two to four percent for money that arrives on Friday makes little sense.
  • There is no fast pay. Relay has no way to speed a payout up. carrier reports
  • Paperwork is automatic. Electronic proof of delivery is generated in the app and at the gate. Nothing gets emailed anywhere. If a document is missing from the trip, open a case rather than sending mail.
  • Payment disputes: thirty days from the trip. After that there is nothing to argue about.

Bonus programs

🏅

Relay Rewards

Tiers starting at Basic, recalculated on the fifth of each month from your score, miles and tenure. Bonuses on promotional loads, discounts on fuel, tires and insurance.

🛡️

Safety Rewards

Opt in and earn one to four cents per mile with no collisions, in tiers by annual mileage. Requires an approved dash camera; Amazon pays for the hardware and the first year of subscription. A collision resets the counter.

🎁

New carrier promos

Periodic sign-up bonuses, for example $1,500 for your first seven loads ahead of Prime Day 2026. Terms appear on relay.amazon.com at registration.

Remember The rate on the card is final and deadhead is never paid. Money arrives Friday for the previous week, with no fees. Detention and cancellation pay only come through cases, and the dispute window is thirty days.

9. How Amazon scores a carrier

What it is

In the broker world your reputation is "people know us". In Relay your reputation is a number from zero to one hundred and a grade from F to A+, recalculated over your last six active weeks. That number decides which loads you see, whether you win auctions and whether you stay in the system at all. A dispatcher who does not look at the scorecard daily is flying blind.

The four metrics, and the rule that matters

MetricWhat it measuresWhat breaks itWho owns it
On-timeArriving inside the window at every stop, measured by the Check In timestamp.Lateness, weighted by duration: 1–60 minutes ×1, 61–240 ×2, 241–600 ×4, 601+ ×8.The driver taps on time, the dispatcher plans with slack.
AcceptanceHow much of what you booked you actually hauled.Cancellations and expired loads. Blocks: more than 24 hours ×1, under 24 hours or after the start ×4. Contracts: 3+ weeks ×1, closer ×2.The dispatcher, entirely.
App usageHalf the app steps completed (Check In, Pick Up, Drop Off), half location availability on the road.Skipped steps, a closed app, battery saver, "forgot to tap".Driver plus dispatcher, through phone settings and map checks.
Disruption-freeNo incidents: wrong trailer, swapped driver, yard violations, complaints, damage.Incidents recorded by Amazon or the facility.Both.

What each grade unlocks

GradeWhat you get
AnyThe load board, Post A Truck and Relay Rewards.
C+ and upPlus block auctions (reefer auctions start at B).
B and upPlus multi-week contracts.
B+ and upPlus early access to new loads and auctions.
A and A+Plus price alerts, an edge in auctions and counter-offers on urgent loads through the assistant.
Below C+Auctions close and contracts you already hold can be pulled. Lower still, suspension is in view.

Amazon does not publish the exact boundaries. Carriers report A from 98%, B from 90%, C from 70%. The score appears after your first ten loads. Cancellations by Amazon do not count. Questionable entries are disputed in the Performance section of the site, not from the app.

The second scorecard: safety

Next to the performance score sits the safety scorecard. It shows your CSA percentiles and, since 2026, your violation rate on roadside inspections: no more than 35% for drivers and 50% for vehicles. Unlike performance, this cannot be fixed in six weeks: the window is twelve months and the violation rate cannot be appealed. The only levers are clean inspections, which grow the denominator, and DataQ challenges on questionable violations, which shrink the numerator.

The dispatcher's daily score routine

  • 🌅Morning: the lowest metric and the forecast▼

    Do not look at the overall number, look at which of the four is currently lowest. That is where the day's attention goes. The forecast shows where things are heading if nothing changes.

  • 📋Yesterday's new entries▼

    Every late arrival, skipped step and incident, each with a trip ID. Dispute the questionable ones today, attaching yesterday's support cases.

  • 📱App usage by driver▼

    If the metric is falling, find whose trip is dragging it: the timelines show where location was missing or a step was skipped. Talk to that driver, do not send a blanket "everyone turn the app on".

  • 🛡️Weekly: the safety scorecard▼

    New inspections, percentile movement, violation rate. Challenge a questionable violation through DataQ immediately.

  • 🎯Before every booking: "is there a driver right now?"▼

    Acceptance is the one metric the dispatcher controls completely. One expired load a week out of twenty is five percent gone and a grade lost.

Remember Your score is the worst of four metrics over six active weeks. Auctions at C+, contracts at B. The safety scorecard runs on a twelve-month window and cannot be appealed. Check both every day.

10. A dispatcher's week with Amazon

Everything above, folded into a schedule. Working Relay is a cycle: auctions a week ahead, assignments a day ahead, hour-by-hour control on the day of the block, then paperwork, disputes and the payment check.

A week out and earlier

Mon–Tue
Auctions. Check auctions one to six weeks out for your fleet. Price a block against the worst case: full mileage, driver hours, deadhead out and home. Place bids. See which contracts are ending and rebid them.
Wed
Truck calendar. Put won blocks, board loads and broker freight into one calendar for next week. Check for overlaps. Decide where to post a truck to fill the gaps.
Thu–Fri
People and equipment. Every block needs a driver in Active status and a tractor entered in Relay. Check hours of service: a thirty-seven-hour solo block needs a fresh clock. New drivers must have finished the training videos.
Fri
Money. Reconcile last week's payment against the trips. Anything off goes into a payment dispute inside thirty days. Look at the score forecast.

Twenty-four hours before a block

−24 h
Trips. Every trip in the block has a driver and a tractor. Open each one: stops, windows, trailer type, live or drop. Red flags: a night delivery station, a busy facility in peak season, an unfamiliar code.
−24 h
Cancel now if you must. This is the moment: over twenty-four hours it counts once, under it counts four times. Any doubt about the driver or the truck gets settled here.
−12 h
The driver's briefing from the template in part 2: addresses, windows, where to stage, what to photograph, your number.
−12 h
The driver's phone. App updated, location Always, charger in the cab. For a new driver, a video call to check the settings together.
−2 h
Trips again. Amazon's cancellations show up here before the driver's five-hour alert. If one landed, pull a replacement off the board from the same point.

The day of the block, by the clock

Start −1 h
The driver taps Begin work. A dot appears on the tracking map. No dot means a phone call: app, location.
Pickup −45 m
Truck at the facility, on the staging lot. Remind him: at the gate, tap Check In.
Pickup
Confirm the Check In timestamp is inside the window on the trip timeline. Then Pick Up, verify trailer ID, inspection, seal. Wait for the seal photo.
+30 m
Still on site? Ask why. +90 m open a case "waiting since". +120 m open the second one for detention.
In transit
Watch the trip colour on the map. Red means call the driver and open a "running late, new ETA" case. Make sure he has seen the weather alert.
Delivery −30 m
If the block continues, check the trailer status for the next leg. A problem visible now is a case opened now.
Delivery
Check In, Drop Off, Trailer Detach, Check Out, all before the gate. Timeline screenshot from the driver. Pull a return load if the block is done.
End
Is an Amazon trailer still hooked to your truck? If so, open a case before the shift ends. Every trip in the block should show Completed.

After the block

+1 day
Paperwork. Proof of delivery present in the trip. Timeline screenshots of any delay filed with the trip. Cases opened for detention, cancellation or layover where there are grounds.
+1 day
Scorecard. Any new entries: lateness, a skipped step, lost tracking. Dispute the questionable ones immediately with the day's cases attached.
+1–2 days
Debrief the driver. Not "why were you late" but "at which step did we lose the time and what changes". One point at a time.
Friday
Payment. Pay week runs Sunday to Saturday, money lands the following Friday. Reconcile the amount against the trips. Detention promised in a case but not paid becomes a payment dispute.

11. Relay against broker freight

✓ FOR RELAY

  • A direct shipper: no double brokering, no "the broker did not pay".
  • Weekly payment with no fees and no factoring.
  • Amazon trailers: power only with no trailer costs of your own.
  • One-button booking, no calls and no rate confirmations. Reload suggestions out of the delivery point.
  • Contract blocks: a predictable week, paid for time even when the freight is light.
  • Steady volume, peaking from October through January and around Prime Day.
  • Tracking and paperwork inside one app; proof of delivery does not go missing.

✗ AGAINST

  • Rates run fifteen to thirty percent under the broker spot market, with no negotiation.
  • Deadhead is never paid.
  • Tight windows: thirty to forty-five minutes late already costs score.
  • Two to four hour queues at peak, capped detention paid only through disputes.
  • The score is the worst metric, so one weak driver drags the whole company.
  • The inspection violation rate has no appeal, and suspensions can be formal.
  • No "guy you know": only cases and support.
🎯
The mix most small carriers settle on: twenty to fifty percent of the fleet's work in Relay, with contract blocks as the skeleton of the week and the board for backhauls and gaps, and the rest in broker freight at a higher margin. Relay gives stability and cash flow, brokers give the upside. A dispatcher who can run both is worth more.

Glossary

Amazon RelayAmazon's platform for carriers: the site for dispatchers and the app for drivers, at relay.amazon.com.
MC and DOT numbersIssued by the federal regulator. MC is the authority to haul for hire across state lines, DOT is the company registration number.
FMCSAThe Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, whose databases Amazon checks.
CSA and BASICThe regulator's safety measurement system and its percentile categories. Higher percentile is worse.
FC, SC, DSAmazon facility types: fulfillment center, sortation center, delivery station.
Facility code (ONT8, JFK8)Three letters for the nearest airport plus the facility number in that area.
Power onlyYou supply tractor and driver, Amazon supplies the trailer and the freight.
Drop and hookDrop the trailer you brought, hook the next one. No waiting to be loaded.
Live loadThe driver waits at a door while the trailer is loaded.
Load boardThe list of Amazon loads on the Relay site.
Book now, BidBook instantly at the posted rate, or offer your own price.
Post A TruckAdvertise an available truck with a minimum rate; the system finds and books the load.
BlockPaid truck time — 13, 37 hours or more — inside which Amazon runs several trips. Paid in full.
Auction and contractBlocks are auctioned weeks ahead. A won auction becomes a contract: the same blocks every week.
TripOne load or one leg inside a block, with an ID like T-…
Gate passThe QR code in the app you show at the entrance scanner.
Check In, Pick Up, Drop OffThe app steps at every facility. Each leaves a timestamp; Check In decides on-time.
Trailer inspectionMandatory photos of the Amazon trailer in the app before departure: sides, tires, lights, seal.
SealThe single-use lock on the trailer doors with a number that must match the paperwork. Drivers never touch it.
Support, ROCAmazon's round-the-clock service: cases on the site, the assistant in the app, a live operator.
Performance scoreZero to one hundred with a grade from F to A+. Equals the worst of four metrics over six active weeks.
The four metricsOn-time, acceptance, app usage, disruption-free.
Safety scorecardCSA percentiles plus the violation rate on inspections. Twelve-month window, no appeal.
Detention, TONU, layoverPay for waiting at a facility, for a load cancelled after you rolled, and for a forced overnight. In Relay all three come through cases.
Trailer interchangeThe $50,000 insurance line covering somebody else's trailer. Without it Relay will not accept you.
Relay Rewards, Safety RewardsBonus programs: tiers by score and tenure, and cents per mile for collision-free driving with a dash camera.
Plan of actionThe appeal document after a suspension: what happened, what has been fixed, what changes.
