Chapter 1. At the facilityThe trailer is not ready
The dispatcherA case at once: trip number, spot number, arrival time. Ask for another trailer or for the wait to be confirmed
To the driver“Stay put, do not go hunting the trailer around the yard. Photo of the empty spot and of the screen. A message every 30 minutes”
What it costs · risk mediumBeing late to the next stop through the facility’s fault can be disputed if a timed case exists. Detention needs its own case
How to prevent itAt facilities where this keeps happening, build in an extra 45 minutes
Chapter 1. At the facilityNo empty trailer
The dispatcherA case: “no empty trailer at the facility, the next trip is such-and-such”. Wait for a written answer
To the driver“Ask the yard staff whether there are empties in another zone, write down the name. Do not cruise the yard yourself”
What it costs · risk mediumReassigned through Amazon’s fault and your score is untouched. Leave without instruction and lose the trip, and the refusal is yours
How to prevent itCheck the next trip’s trailer status an hour before the drop
Chapter 1. At the facilityWaiting more than two hours
The dispatcherAt 1.5 hours, a case: “waiting since…”. At 2 hours, a second one about detention. Payment disputes: 30 days
To the driver“Do not close the app. A screenshot of the trip timeline now and again when you leave”
What it costs · risk lowAmazon publishes no official amounts. Carriers report: two hours free, anything beyond it through a case
How to prevent itIn peak season, price a live load by time, not by miles
Chapter 1. At the facilityThe seal does not match
The dispatcherA case immediately, before departure: trip number, trailer number, the seal number on the document and on the trailer, photos
To the driver“Do not touch the seal. Close-up photos of the seal, the document and the trailer number. Stay put until I write back”
What it costs · risk highLeaving with the wrong seal puts the cargo-integrity question on you, with a possible claim and a recorded incident
How to prevent itChecking the seal is part of the trailer inspection. Never skip it
Chapter 1. At the facilityThe trailer is damaged
The dispatcherCheck the damage is in the inspection. A case with photos. Not roadworthy — demand a replacement trailer
To the driver“Every bit of damage goes into the inspection with a photo, even small ones. If it affects safety, do not leave”
What it costs · risk mediumDamage you did not record is charged to you. Running unroadworthy means a violation at inspection, and that cannot be appealed
How to prevent itTen minutes for the inspection every time, even on a clean trailer
Chapter 1. At the facilityNo spot or door assigned
The dispatcherA case with the arrival time and the current time. The assistant in the app is the fastest route
To the driver“Ask the yard staff, park where they say, do not take another spot. Screenshot the instruction”
What it costs · risk lowUsually only lost time. If it breaks the next stop, the case already exists and the delay can be disputed
How to prevent itKnow the facility: many have a separate staging area
Chapter 1. At the facilityThe gate is shut at the appointed time
The dispatcherA case and a live operator: ask for a new window and for the facility’s fault to be recorded. Waiting till morning — document it for pay
To the driver“Tap Check In inside the window regardless. Photo of the closed gate and the hours sign. Wait on a permitted lot”
What it costs · risk mediumWith a timestamp inside the window and a case, it is disputable. Without the timestamp the lateness is yours
How to prevent itPut gate hours into your own facility table
Chapter 1. At the facilityThe app would not take the check-in
The dispatcherA case immediately with the error screenshot. The trip timeline shows the location at the facility — that is the argument
To the driver“Screenshot the error with the clock in frame. Restart the app, check location. Give the guard the load number and the time”
What it costs · risk highLateness is counted to the moment the timestamp appears: under an hour is the normal penalty, under four it doubles, beyond that more
How to prevent itThe driver’s rule number one: reach the gate — tap it
Chapter 1. At the facilityHe left without tapping Drop Off
The dispatcherClose by — bring him back and tap it. Far — a case: departure time, trailer number, the spot, photos
To the driver“Do not start the next stop until I sort this out. What time did you leave? Do you have a photo of the trailer in its spot?”
What it costs · risk mediumAn unfinished step costs app-usage score, delays the delivery document, and the next trip may not open
How to prevent itThe buttons are tapped before leaving the gate
Chapter 1. At the facilityThe selfie fails verification
The dispatcherSame driver — a case asking for a manual check. Different driver — reassign the trip immediately and state it
To the driver“Take the cap and glasses off, face the light, try again. Do not ask a colleague to stand in”
What it costs · risk highAn identity mismatch blocks the driver and is recorded against the company; a repeat switches the account off

Chapter 2. Before and after the runThe load expired with no driver
The dispatcherCannot be fixed. If the load is still on the board, book it again and assign at once — but the first refusal is already recorded
To the driver—
What it costs · risk highAcceptance falls. The closer to pickup this happened, the harder it hits the score
How to prevent itNo driver right now — do not book; post your truck to the board instead

Chapter 2. Before and after the runAmazon cancelled the trip
The dispatcherThe score is untouched. Check trips in the morning. Cancelled after arrival — a case under payments
To the driver“This is not on us. Do not leave, I am looking for a replacement out of this point. If you arrived, send me a screenshot of the timeline”
What it costs · risk lowCarriers report about $175 for a late cancellation. A partly cancelled block is still paid in full
How to prevent itDo not run 200 empty miles for a single load with no backhaul
Chapter 2. Before and after the runOne truck on two trips
The dispatcherDecide at once which trip is real and hand the second back today — while the start is more than 24 hours away
To the driver“Your trip is such-and-such, I am taking the second one off. Change nothing in the app”
What it costs · risk highOne refusal at the penalty for its timing. Tomorrow the same refusal costs four times more
How to prevent itA truck calendar outside Relay, checked before every booking
Chapter 2. Before and after the runLate because of weather or traffic
The dispatcherA case as soon as the ETA slips past the window: reason, new time, screenshot of the weather alert or the traffic
To the driver“Safety beats the window. Drive legally, I have already written to support”
What it costs · risk mediumThe penalty scales with duration: under an hour ordinary, under four double, beyond that four and eight times
How to prevent itWeather alerts in the app refresh every 10 minutes — check them before the start
Chapter 2. Before and after the runA breakdown and a roadside inspection
🔧 Breakdown
A live operator and a case. Do not unhook the Amazon trailer without being told. The options: a replacement tractor, reassigning the load, a tow by agreement
👮 Roadside inspection
A case with the expected delay, write down the report number. Every violation goes into the company’s numbers
Challenge questionable violations with the regulator at once, before the numbers are recalculated
Chapter 3. The company accountThe insurance lapsed
The dispatcherTo the agent: a new certificate with every line, today. A case: “insurance renewed, document sent, please expedite”
To the driver“One to three days off, the board is blocked for us”
What it costs · risk highLost revenue for the idle days, possible refusals on the loads that fell off, lost contracts if the freeze drags on
How to prevent itA reminder 14 days before the policy ends and the list of requirements to the agent in writing
Chapter 3. The company accountThe score dropped below the line
The dispatcherFind the worst of the four metrics and work on it specifically. Questionable entries — dispute on the site, not in the app
To the driverBe specific: “Our app-usage metric has slipped. For two weeks: app open, every step tapped”
What it costs · risk highBelow the line the auctions disappear, won contracts can be pulled, and beyond that comes the risk of suspension
How to prevent itLook at the worst metric every day rather than waiting for a letter from Amazon
Chapter 3. The company accountThe account was switched off
The dispatcherRead the reason. Appeal through support with a plan: what happened, what is fixed, the measures going forward
To the driverBe honest about the timeline. If the cause is one specific driver, the decision belongs to the owner
What it costs · risk highDowntime from a few weeks to a permanent shutdown
How to prevent itNever open a second account for the same team: both get found and closed
Chapter 3. The company accountA call “from Amazon Relay”
The dispatcherSay nothing. Details to relay-network-reports@amazon.com. Access already shared — change the password and revoke permissions
To the driver“Never read out a code from a text message and never install a second app because a caller asked you to”
What it costs · risk highSomebody else’s loads under your number, somebody else’s drivers under your account, a shutdown for fraud
How to prevent itA personal login for every employee and two-step sign-in
