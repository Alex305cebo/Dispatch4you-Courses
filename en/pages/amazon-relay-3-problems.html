7. When things go wrong

One rule that covers all of them

The driver stops and calls the dispatcher. The dispatcher opens a support case with the trip number and the exact time, while the problem is still happening. The driver then acts only on the instruction that comes back. Every screen and every clock gets captured in a screenshot. A driver improvising almost always costs more than an hour of waiting.

Below are twenty-two situations, all laid out the same way: what the dispatcher does, what to tell the driver, what it costs. The tag on the right shows how dangerous it is for the company account. Click a situation to open it.

At the facility

🚫 The trailer is not ready: still being loaded or not in the slotMEDIUM
DispatcherOpen a case immediately: trip number, slot, check-in time, "trailer not ready". Ask for either a different trailer or written confirmation of the wait with pay. Watch the next stop's window: if the wait kills it, say so in the same case before it happens.
Driver"Stay in your slot or on the staging lot, do not go hunting for a trailer around the yard. Photograph the empty slot with its number and the app screen. Message me every thirty minutes."
What it costsA late arrival at the next stop caused by the facility can usually be disputed if the case has timestamps. Detention comes through a separate case after two hours of waiting, per carrier reports, and is not automatic.
PreventionAt facilities where this repeats, build in an extra forty-five minutes. Keep your own facility table with typical wait times.
📭 No empty trailer for the next leg (power only)MEDIUM
DispatcherCase: "no empty trailer at [code], next trip [id]". Possible answers from Amazon: wait, bobtail to the next stop, or the trip gets reassigned. Without a written instruction the driver goes nowhere; leaving on his own can be recorded as a refused trip.
Driver"Ask the yard worker whether there are empties in another zone and write down his name. Do not drive around the yard. Wait for my message."
What it costsIf Amazon reassigns the trip, your score is untouched. If the driver leaves without instruction and the trip expires, the rejection is yours.
PreventionAn hour before the drop, check the trailer status of the next trip in Trips. If it shows unavailable, call support early.
⏱️ Live load running past two hoursLOW
DispatcherNinety minutes after Check In, open a case: "driver waiting since hh:mm at door N". At two hours, open a second one under Payments asking for detention. Collect the Check In screenshot, the time at the door and the departure time. A payment dispute can be filed within thirty days of the trip.
Driver"Do not close the app. Screenshot the timeline now and again when you pull out. If they move you to another door, photograph the time."
What it costsAmazon does not publish free time or detention rates. Carriers report roughly two hours free, a capped hourly rate after that, and payment only when the app steps are properly timestamped.
PreventionPrice live loads at busy facilities by the hour, not by the mile. Budget two to three hours on site.
🔒 The seal does not match the paperwork, or it is damagedHIGH
DispatcherCase immediately, before the truck moves: trip number, trailer number, the seal number on the paperwork and the one on the trailer, photos. Wait for the instruction: reseal by the facility, reissue the paperwork, or swap the trailer.
Driver"Do not touch the seal. Close-up photo of the seal, the paperwork in the app and the trailer number. Stay where you are until I write."
What it costsLeaving with a mismatched seal means the cargo integrity question at delivery lands on the carrier. A cargo claim and an incident on the score are both possible. Standing still for an hour is cheaper.
PreventionSeal verification is part of the trailer inspection in the app. Never skip it.
🛠️ The Amazon trailer is damaged or unsafeMEDIUM
DispatcherConfirm the driver captured the damage in the app inspection. Case: "trailer [number] damaged: description, photos". If it is not roadworthy — tire, lights, brakes — demand a replacement rather than accepting "just run it".
Driver"Every bit of damage goes into the inspection with photos, even small stuff. If it affects safety, do not roll: a roadside inspection violation goes on our record."
What it costsDamage you did not capture gets charged to you at the drop. Rolling with a defect risks a violation on the vehicle rate, which cannot be appealed.
PreventionTen minutes for the inspection every time. Photograph even a clean trailer: that is the proof it was clean.
🚧 No slot or door assigned, the driver is sent in circlesLOW
DispatcherCase with the check-in time and the current time: "no slot or door assigned". Relay Assistant often clears this in minutes. Log the time in case detention follows.
Driver"Ask a yard worker, park where you are told, do not take somebody else's slot. Screenshot the in-yard instruction."
What it costsUsually just time. If it kills the next stop, the case is already open and the late arrival can be disputed.
PreventionKnow the facility: many have an overflow waiting area a new driver will not find on his own.
🌙 The gate is closed during your window (night or weekend)MEDIUM
DispatcherCase plus a live operator: "facility closed at scheduled window". Ask for a new appointment and confirmation that the delay is the facility's. If the driver waits until morning, log it for a layover claim.
Driver"Tap Check In inside the window anyway, even with the gate shut: it proves you were there on time. Photograph the closed gate and the posted hours. Wait on the approved lot."
What it costsWith a Check In inside the window and a case, it is disputable. Without the tap, the late arrival is yours.
PreventionPut gate hours into your facility table. For night delivery stations, confirm through the app assistant ahead of time.
📵 Check In will not register: no signal, frozen app, or he forgotHIGH
DispatcherIf the driver is on site but the tap did not go through, open a case at once: "driver arrived hh:mm, app check-in failed, screenshot attached". The trip timeline shows whether there was a location ping at the facility, and that is your argument. If he simply forgot, have him tap now and file the explanation immediately: minutes are cheaper than an hour.
Driver"Screenshot the error with the phone clock in frame. Restart the app, check location. If that fails, give the guard the load ID and arrival time and ask him to log it."
What it costsWithout a timestamp, Amazon counts the lateness up to the moment the tap appears: one to sixty minutes counts once, sixty-one to two hundred forty counts double, beyond that four and eight times. Plus a hit to app usage.
PreventionDriver rule number one. Check a new driver's first three trips on the map: did he arrive and tap?
🚪 He left the facility without tapping Drop Off or Check OutMEDIUM
DispatcherIf he is five or ten minutes away, send him back to tap; the location has to match the facility. If he is far, open a case: "driver departed [code] at hh:mm, drop-off step not completed, trailer [number] left in slot X", with a photo if there is one.
Driver"Do not start the next stop until I sort this out. Do you have a photo of the trailer in the slot? What time did you leave?"
What it costsAn unfinished step hurts app usage, delays the proof of delivery and can block the next trip until the previous one closes.
PreventionThe taps happen before the truck rolls out of the gate.
🧑‍✈️ The selfie check fails at the facilityHIGH
DispatcherIf it is the same driver — beard, glasses, bad light — open a case asking for a manual review and have him retry in good light. If the man behind the wheel is not the assigned driver, reassign the trip to the actual driver at once (if he is Active) and say so honestly in the case. A swap discovered later is far worse than any late arrival.
Driver"Take off the cap and glasses, face the light, try again. Do not ask a colleague to show his face: that blocks both of you."
What it costsAn identity mismatch blocks the driver and puts an incident on the carrier. Repeat it and the account gets suspended.
PreventionAssign the driver who is actually going. Swap drivers only by reassigning the trip in the portal.

Before and after the run

⌛ A booked load expired: no driver was assigned in timeHIGH
DispatcherNothing to save here: an expired load counts as a rejection. If it is still on the board you can rebook and assign immediately, but the first rejection is already recorded.
Driver—
What it costsAcceptance drops, and the closer to pickup it happened the harder it hits. Your score is the lowest of four metrics, so one dip in acceptance pulls the whole grade down.
PreventionBook and assign as one action. No driver right now means no booking; post the truck instead.
❌ You have to cancel a booked load or blockHIGH
DispatcherCancel in the portal, as Administrator, as early as possible. Since 2 September 2025: a block cancelled more than twenty-four hours out counts once, under twenty-four hours or after the start counts four times; a contract dropped three or more weeks out counts once, closer than that counts double. Before cancelling, check for a substitute: a second driver, or posting the truck for that slot.
Driver"Do not cancel anything in the app and do not just ignore the trip. I handle the cancellation."
What it costsAcceptance drops in proportion to the multiplier. Cancellations by Amazon itself do not count against you.
PreventionDecide twenty-five hours out, not "we'll see in the morning". Keep a back-up driver for contract blocks.
📦 Amazon cancelled the trip: a day out, five hours out, after arrivalLOW
DispatcherNo score impact. Check Trips in the morning: cancellations appear there before the driver's five-hour alert. If it is cancelled after the driver reached the facility, open a Payments case: "truck arrived hh:mm, load cancelled at hh:mm" and ask for the truck-ordered-not-used payment. The board offers a replacement out of the same point immediately.
Driver"Amazon cancelled it, not our fault. Do not leave yet, I am looking for a replacement from where you are. If you already arrived, screenshot the timeline with the Check In."
What it costsCarrier reports put the payment around $175 for a late cancellation; Amazon does not publish the terms. A partially cancelled block is still paid in full.
PreventionDo not deadhead two hundred miles for a single board load with no return lined up.
🔁 One truck on two overlapping tripsHIGH
DispatcherDecide right now which trip is real and cancel the other while it is still more than twenty-four hours out, when it counts once instead of four times. If both are already close, check a second driver or tractor; otherwise the cancellation is unavoidable.
Driver"Your trip is [id], I am removing the other one. Change nothing in the app."
What it costsOne rejection, weighted by how late you cancelled.
PreventionKeep a truck calendar outside Relay with blocks and board loads, and look at it before every booking.
🌨️ Late because of weather, traffic or a wreckMEDIUM
DispatcherThe moment the projected arrival slips past the window, open a case: "running late", the reason, the new ETA, a screenshot of the weather alert from the app or the traffic map. The lateness still counts, but a dispute with documents sometimes lands.
Driver"Safety beats the window. Screenshot the weather alert from the Relay app. Drive legal, I have already written to support."
What it costsOn-time penalties by duration: one to sixty minutes once, sixty-one to two hundred forty twice, up to six hundred four times, beyond that eight times. Warning early does not change the multiplier but helps the dispute.
PreventionWeather alerts in the app refresh every ten minutes. The dispatcher checks them before a block starts.
🔧 Truck breakdown during a block or on the way to pickupHIGH
DispatcherCall a live operator and open a case: "breakdown at [location], trip [id], Amazon trailer attached or not". Options: swap the tractor and reassign in the trip, let support reassign the load, or tow with the Amazon trailer, which only happens with their approval. An Amazon trailer on the shoulder is their property and their instruction.
Driver"Safe spot, triangles, photos. Do not drop the Amazon trailer without my word. Keep the app open."
What it costsThe disruption-free metric drops and the remaining stops of the block become late or refused. The block is paid for the part performed; the details go through support.
PreventionA real pre-trip. Tires and brakes cause both breakdowns and inspection violations.
👮 A DOT inspection during a blockMEDIUM
DispatcherCase: "DOT inspection in progress at [location], expected delay X". Record the report number. Afterwards read the report: every violation feeds Amazon's violation rate, where an out-of-service item counts double. Dispute questionable violations through DataQ right away, before the numbers are recalculated.
Driver"Stay calm, papers are in order, do not argue with the officer. Send me a photo of the report."
What it costsA clean inspection improves the denominator. A dirty one hurts the numerator, and past three inspections over the threshold the account is cut off with no appeal.
PreventionAnnual inspection current, license and medical card in the cab, clean ELD logs, tidy cab. How hard an officer digs often starts with the first impression.
🚚 Changing tractors in the middle of a blockLOW
DispatcherReassign the tractor in the trip before the truck reaches the next facility, because the gate reads plates. File a note in support about the mid-block change so there are no questions later.
Driver"Do not enter the facility until the tractor is changed in the app. Confirm you see the new plate on the trip."
What it costsWithout the update: refused entry, a late arrival, possibly an incident.
PreventionEnter every company tractor in Relay in advance, not at the moment of the swap.
🅿️ An Amazon trailer is stuck at your yard after the runMEDIUM
DispatcherAn Amazon trailer should not spend the night at your yard. Open a case straight away: "Amazon trailer [number] at our yard after trip [id], request instruction: return to [code] or next load". Usually they give you a return load or a drop address. While it sits with you, it is your responsibility under trailer interchange.
Driver"Close the trailer, do not touch the seal, park it in the secured part of the yard, photos all round."
What it costsProperty claims if anything happens to it, and questions from Amazon if the return drags on without a case.
PreventionWhen planning a block, check how it ends: with a drop at a facility or with a trailer still hooked.

Your company account

🛡️ Insurance lapsed or renewed wrong: the account is frozenHIGH
DispatcherCall the agent: a new certificate with every required line, today. Case in support: "insurance renewed, certificate sent to the vendor at hh:mm, please expedite". Booked loads dropped by the freeze can be counted as rejections, so ask for a review in the same case.
Driver"We are down for one to three days. Do not watch the board, it is locked for us."
What it costsLost revenue for every frozen day, possible rejections on the dropped loads, and lost contracts if the freeze runs long.
PreventionA reminder fourteen days before the policy expires, and Relay's requirements sent to the agent in writing so every renewal carries the right lines.
📉 The score fell below C+: auctions gone, contracts at riskHIGH
DispatcherOpen Scorecard and find the lowest of the four metrics: that number is your score. Work on that one specifically. On-time means earlier departures and timely check-ins; acceptance means booking less and letting nothing expire; app usage means working with drivers on location settings; disruption-free means reviewing incidents. Dispute questionable entries in the portal, not in the app. The window is the last six active weeks, so six clean weeks fully refresh the picture.
DriverBe specific: "our app usage slipped. For two weeks: app stays open, every step gets tapped. I will check every trip."
What it costsBelow C+ the auctions close and contracts you already hold can be pulled. Lower still and part of the board disappears, with suspension in view.
PreventionLook at the lowest metric daily. Do not wait for a letter from Amazon.
⛔ The account is suspendedHIGH
DispatcherRead the reason in the letter: safety numbers over the threshold, an insurance lapse, a no-show, load hopping, multiple accounts, low score. Most reasons can be appealed through Support Center with a plan of action: what happened, what has been fixed, what changes going forward, with documents. The inspection violation rate has no appeal; you wait for old inspections to roll out of the twelve-month window and collect clean ones.
DriverBe honest about the timeline. If the cause is a specific driver, the owner decides what happens next.
What it costsAnything from a few weeks of downtime to permanent removal.
PreventionEverything above. And never open a second account for "another company" with the same people: it gets found and both are closed.
🎭 A call "from Amazon Relay" asking for your login, or selling accountsHIGH
DispatcherAmazon does not call asking you to read out a password and does not sell accounts. Tell them nothing and forward the details to relay-network-reports@amazon.com. If somebody in the company already shared access, change the password, revoke access in the portal and open a case.
Driver"Never read out the code from a Relay text and never install a 'second app' because someone on the phone asked you to."
What it costsSomebody else's loads under your MC number, somebody else's drivers under your account, and suspension for fraud.
PreventionA personal login for every employee and two-factor sign-in on the company account.

Try it yourself

Three situations from practice. Pick an answer and the explanation appears at once.

1. The driver reaches the gate at 22:10, the window runs to 23:00. The guard asks him to wait and walks away. What should he do right now?

2. Tuesday, 3 pm. The driver assigned to Wednesday's 2 pm block is sick and there is no replacement yet. What does the dispatcher do?

3. During the inspection the seal number differs from the paperwork by one digit. The delivery window is two hours away and the run takes ninety minutes. What now?

Remember The driver stops and calls. The dispatcher writes with the trip number and the exact time while the problem is still live. Cancellations happen as early as possible and only in the portal. The most dangerous things for the company: expired loads, a swapped driver, leaving with the wrong seal and an insurance lapse.
