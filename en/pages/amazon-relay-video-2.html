Chapter 2. Taking a loadThe message to the driver
The message to the driver before the run
Sergey, this is Thursday’s run. Facility CDW5 in Carteret, window 22:00–23:00. Get there by 21:15, park on the staging lot to the right of the entrance, do not approach the gate before 21:45. The moment you join the gate queue, tap Check In in the app. Amazon trailer, drop and hook. Do the trailer inspection: photos from every side, the seal, the tires. If the seal number does not match the document, do not leave — call me. Then JFK8 on Staten Island by 02:30, the toll bridges are already in the rate. I am reachable all night.

Chapter 3. The driver at the facilityHow to write a case, and one real example
What a case must contain
The trip number and the load number The driver’s name and the tractor number The facility code and which stop it is What happened, with exact times: “arrived 02:21, the spot was empty, an employee confirmed at 02:50, still waiting, it is now 03:40” What you are asking for: an instruction for the driver, detention pay, a new appointment time Attachments: a screenshot of the app, a photo of the spot, the name of the facility employee
A case from practice
Wrote the next day, “we waited a long time yesterday” — detention not confirmed
Wrote after 1.5 hours of waiting with screenshots — detention paid
