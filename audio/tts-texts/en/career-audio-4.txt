A ninety-day plan to launch your dispatch business. Follow this and in ninety days you have a working business with real clients.

Weeks one and two: training and setup. Finish the course. Register the LLC. Get your EIN. Open the bank account. Subscribe to DAT. Write your dispatch agreement. Set up email and phone. Prepare your carrier packet.

Weeks three and four: find your first client. Look for owner-operators — Facebook groups, Instagram, TikTok, Craigslist. Offer a free trial week. The goal is one or two carriers on trial. Then show results: find them good loads.

Weeks five through eight: your first loads. Dispatch those first clients. Learn by doing. Build relationships with brokers. Collect reviews and ask for referrals. Goal: three to five carriers.

Weeks nine through twelve: scale. Add a second load board, Truckstop. Streamline your process. Start keeping a CRM. Goal: five to eight carriers, and $3,000 to $5,000 a month.

Months four through six: stabilize. Build relationships with ten to fifteen brokers. Add a TMS. Raise your fee to seven or eight percent. Goal: eight to twelve carriers, $5,000 to $8,000 a month.

Months seven through twelve: grow. Hire an assistant. Expand your base. Goal: fifteen carriers or more, $8,000 to $15,000 a month.
