The platforms, compared in detail. There are six main ones, each with its strengths, its pricing and its audience. Most professional dispatchers run two or three at once for maximum coverage.

Start with the leader: DAT. The undisputed market leader since 1978, with the largest freight database in North America — more than five hundred million loads a year pass through it. If you are serious about this work, DAT is mandatory: ninety percent of professional dispatchers use it as their main tool.

DAT RateView is the feature competitors cannot match at this quality. It shows the average rate on any lane over the last fifteen days, based on actual transactions. That is your main argument in a negotiation: "the average on this lane is $2.45 a mile according to DAT," and the broker knows you are not bluffing.

Pricing. DAT One at $45 a month gives you basic load search and truck posting. DAT Power at $150 gives full access to RateView, lane analytics, broker scores and hot market maps. For a beginner, DAT One is enough. Once you have three or more drivers, move to Power.

The advantages: the largest load base in the U.S., RateView, and the best mobile app with push notifications. The drawbacks: the most expensive subscription on the market, an interface that can overwhelm beginners, and heavy competition, because every dispatcher is there.

Next, Truckstop.com. The second largest platform, founded in 1995, historically strong with owner-operators and small carriers. In 2021 Truckstop merged with Internet Truckstop, which grew its load base considerably.

Its key feature is Truckstop rate analysis — DAT's RateView equivalent, but calculated differently. Many dispatchers compare both to get a truer picture. Truckstop also offers Book It Now: instant booking without calling the broker.

Who is it for? An excellent second platform alongside DAT. Some brokers post only on Truckstop. Advantages: Book It Now, good rate analytics, a simpler interface than DAT. Drawbacks: fewer loads than DAT, and less detailed broker scores.

Now 123Loadboard, the budget alternative for beginners and owner-operators, from $29 a month. It aggregates freight from several sources, including DAT and Truckstop, which gives broad access for less money. Its distinctive feature: a broker credit check included in the subscription. On DAT that costs extra, and for a beginner who does not yet know which brokers to trust, it matters.

A strategy. Start with 123Loadboard at $29 a month. Learn the basics of searching, reading postings and reading rates. After a month or two, when you know you need more, move to DAT. Many dispatchers keep 123Loadboard as a backup even after switching.

Advantages: the lowest price on the market, credit checks included, aggregation from several sources. Drawbacks: weaker rate analytics, some loads appear with a delay, and fewer search filters.

The conclusion: run several platforms at once for the widest view of the market.
