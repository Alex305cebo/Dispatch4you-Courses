A professional dispatcher does not improvise. They follow a system. When a problem hits, you do not have time to work out what to do — you need the routine ready. Here is a universal six-step response.

Step one: assess. Thirty seconds. What happened? Who is affected? How urgent is it? Sort it by level. Red, critical: act now. Yellow, serious: solve within the hour. Green, routine: solve within the day. Reading the level fast is what separates an experienced dispatcher from a beginner.

Step two: safety. One minute. If the problem is on the road, make sure the driver is safe. Hazards, triangles, a safe place to stop. "Are you safe?" is always the first question. Only after you have the answer do you move on to solving anything.

Step three: notify. Five minutes. Who gets the call? The broker, always. The insurer, if there is damage or an accident. Your factoring company, if it is a payment problem. ERS, if it is a breakdown. The rule: everyone affected hears from you in the first fifteen minutes. Bad news does not improve with age.

Step four: document, in parallel with solving. While you work the problem, the driver documents it: photos, timestamps, GPS, ELD records, notes on the BOL. Any fact that is not recorded is lost. A timestamped photo is proof you cannot argue with. Your word without paperwork is nothing.

Step five: solve. Breakdown: ERS plus a backup plan. Damage: photos plus the insurer. Detention: timestamps plus an invoice. Non-payment: factoring plus the bond claim. Every problem has its own routine.

Step six: close it out. The problem is solved, but the work is not finished. Send every document: POD, invoice, detention receipt, damage report. Tell the broker how it ended. Thank the driver. And write down the lesson: what went wrong, and how to prevent it next time. The best dispatchers keep a problem log. Six months in, you have a playbook for anything.

The numbers back this up. A dispatcher working from a system solves problems three times faster and loses five times less money than one who improvises. The system removes panic and puts clear steps in its place.
