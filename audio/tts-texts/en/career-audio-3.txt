How to launch a dispatch business for two thousand dollars.

Dispatching is one of the cheapest businesses to start. You do not need a $150,000 truck. You do not need a warehouse. You do not need an FMCSA license, because you are not a broker — you are a dispatcher representing the carrier's interests.

Here is what the setup takes. Legal: register an LLC in your state, which runs $50 to $500. Get an EIN, free at IRS.gov. Open a business bank account. Create a dispatch agreement, the contract with your carrier — templates are available online. The whole process takes one to three days.

Tools: a load board, DAT, at $45 a month. A business phone, Google Voice, $10 to $30. Google Workspace, $6 a month. Optionally a website, anywhere from nothing to $200.

Total to get started: $600 to $1,800. That is all of it.

Compare that. Starting your own trucking company with a truck: $30,000 to $200,000. Starting a freight brokerage: $10,000 to $50,000. Starting a dispatch company: $600 to $1,800. It is the lowest barrier to entry in the whole industry.

Your only real asset at the start is knowledge and the ability to find good loads. That is exactly what this course gave you.
