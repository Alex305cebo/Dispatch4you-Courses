A dispatcher sends fifty to a hundred emails a day. Templates save two to three hours daily and keep your writing professional. Here are the six that matter.

One: the load inquiry. Your first email to a broker. Subject line: "Available for Chicago to Atlanta, dry van 53." In the body: the load number from the board, the route, your equipment type, your MC and DOT numbers, proof of insurance, and when you are available. Send it within five minutes of spotting the load, with everything included the first time. It saves the broker's time and it tells them you know what you are doing.

Two: pickup confirmation. Subject: "Pickup confirmed, load number." In the body: pickup time, address, that the piece count and weight match the BOL, that there is no damage, and the ETA to delivery. Send it right after loading. The broker sees a professional, and that builds trust.

Three: the in-transit update. Subject: "In transit update, load number." In the body: current location, status, ETA unchanged. Send it even when everything is fine. No news does not read as good news to a broker — it reads as silence, and silence makes them nervous.

Four: the delay notification. This is the most important template you have. Subject: "Urgent, delay on load." In the body: the reason, the old ETA, the new ETA, and where the driver stands. Send it the moment you know. A broker will forgive a late load. They will not forgive a surprise.

Five: POD plus invoice, after delivery. Subject: "Delivered, POD and invoice attached." In the body: delivery time, who signed for it, no damage, and two attachments — the signed BOL and the invoice. Send it the same day. The faster the POD goes out, the faster the payment clock starts and the faster you get paid.

Six: the payment follow-up. Send it five days before the deadline. Polite but firm, with the POD and invoice attached again for convenience. If you use a factoring company, they handle this for you.

The golden rule for every email: short, specific, professional. No emoji, no slang. Every email you send is your face in front of that broker.
