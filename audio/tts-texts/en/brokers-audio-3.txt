So we have covered the top ten U.S. brokers. Now the part that matters most: how to be sure a broker is reliable before you trust them with a load.

Checking through your factoring company is not just convenience. It is your first line of defense against fraud, and it is where verification should start.

Verification takes five to ten minutes. Those minutes can save you $2,500 on a single load. The industry loses more than $100 million a year to fraud, and the fastest, most reliable check is your factoring company's website. If you work with factoring, start there.

If you use OTR Solutions, RTS Financial, Apex Capital, Thunder Funding or another, each has a free broker verification tool on their site. Enter the broker's MC number and within thirty seconds you see their credit rating, payment history, days to pay, and a recommendation.

Why does this matter so much? Factoring companies work with thousands of brokers daily and hold a current database on every one. They know who pays on time, who runs late, and who does not pay at all. That information updates in real time — considerably more current than DAT or Carrier411.

How it works: go to your factoring company's site, find the broker check, credit check or verify broker section, enter the MC number, get an instant result. Most also show whether they would buy that invoice. If factoring refuses, that is a first-level red flag.

Where to find it: OTR Solutions has broker check at otrsolutions.com. RTS Financial has credit check at rtsinc.com. Apex Capital has broker verification at apexcapitalcorp.com. Thunder Funding has broker check at thunderfunding.com. All free for their clients.

One important change in 2025: FMCSA is moving to a single USDOT number system. MC numbers are gradually being replaced by USDOT numbers with a broker designation. Both remain valid for now, but the MC number will eventually disappear. Check both at safer.fmcsa.dot.gov.

Remember: a factoring check is thirty seconds that can save thousands of dollars. Before you spend time on any other step, check the broker through factoring. If factoring says no, no further verification is needed. Use this tool before every new broker, without exception.
