Let's go through every tool on a dispatcher's desk. Understanding each one is understanding how the whole operation works.

DAT load board. The main freight exchange in the United States, with more than five hundred thousand loads a day. It includes RateView for analyzing market rates, saved searches with alerts, and broker scores that rate reliability. It costs $45 to $150 a month depending on the plan.

The TMS. The center of everything. A dispatch board, load management from booking to delivery, driver tracking, automatic invoice creation, reporting — all in one interface. Prices run $30 to $500 a month depending on fleet size.

The ELD, the electronic logging device. Motive is the most popular. It records hours of service automatically, gives you real-time GPS tracking, generates driver reports, and sends alerts when a driver is approaching their limits. It costs $25 to $45 a month per truck.

Google Maps, for route planning and ETA with traffic. Free. For accurate truck routing, professionals use PC*Miler, which accounts for height and weight restrictions and legal truck routes.

Factoring. A financial tool that gets you paid fast. Instead of waiting thirty to forty-five days, you have money in twenty-four to forty-eight hours, for a fee of one to five percent of the invoice. Common companies: OTR Solutions, RTS and Apex.

QuickBooks. The small business accounting standard in the U.S. Invoices, expense tracking, tax reports, bank reconciliation. Twenty-five to eighty dollars a month.

WhatsApp and Zello, for talking to drivers. WhatsApp for text and photos. Zello works like a push-to-talk radio, and drivers love it. Both are free.

DocuSign, for electronic signatures on rate cons and contracts. A driver signs from their phone in thirty seconds. Ten to twenty-five dollars a month.

Google Drive, for cloud storage. A folder per load — rate con, BOL, POD, invoice — reachable from any device. Fifteen gigabytes free.

A fuel card, for discounts of five to fifty cents a gallon. Free to carry; it only saves you money.

And ChatGPT as an AI assistant, for email templates, replies to brokers and drafting documents. It saves one to two hours a day on routine writing.
